About This Project

Project Seva is InAmigos Foundation's core initiative focused on providing food and clothing to underprivileged and homeless communities across India. Through regular food distribution drives, the project ensures that no one goes hungry, especially in vulnerable urban and rural pockets. Volunteers and interns organize collection, packaging, and distribution efforts, often reaching out during festivals and emergencies to maximize impact. The project also collects and distributes clothing donations to those in need, particularly during winter months.

Our Impact

Regular food distribution drives reaching homeless and underprivileged individuals; winter clothing donation campaigns; community-driven volunteer participation; emergency relief support during crises.
