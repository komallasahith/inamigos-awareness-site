About This Project

Project Prakriti is InAmigos Foundation's environmental conservation initiative focused on protecting and restoring the natural environment through community action. Central to this project are tree plantation drives conducted especially during the monsoon season, along with awareness campaigns on sustainability, waste reduction, and climate action. Volunteers actively participate in planting saplings, cleaning up public spaces, and educating communities on eco-friendly practices.

Our Impact

Large-scale tree plantation drives across regions; environmental awareness and climate action campaigns; community clean-up initiatives; long-term commitment to sustainability and conservation.
