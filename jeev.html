About This Project

Project Jeev is InAmigos Foundation's animal welfare initiative focused on the rescue, care, and rehabilitation of stray and injured animals. The project addresses the urgent need for compassionate animal care in communities where strays often face neglect, injury, or abandonment. Volunteers respond to rescue calls, coordinate with local veterinarians for treatment, and work on rehoming or rehabilitating animals back into safe environments.

Our Impact

Rescue and medical treatment for injured and abandoned animals; sterilization and vaccination awareness efforts; rehabilitation and rehoming support; community sensitization on animal welfare and coexistence.
