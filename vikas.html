About This Project

Project Vikas is InAmigos Foundation's flagship skill development and employability initiative, running for over four years and having provided 1,000+ internship opportunities to more than 30,000 interns across domains like Web Development, Digital Marketing, Content Writing, Graphic Design, Video Editing, and more. The project bridges the gap between education and employability by offering practical, hands-on internship experience, webinars, and career guidance to students and young professionals nationwide.

Our Impact

1,000+ internship opportunities provided; 100,000+ interns engaged, 30,000+ onboarded; hands-on experience across multiple career domains; webinars, seminars, and career skill-building sessions for holistic development.
